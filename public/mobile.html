How to Use V-Share

Quick guide to transferring files seamlessly

1 Pair Phone with Your PC
Open V-Share on your PC screen. Tap Scan QR on this phone and point your camera at the PC QR code (or type the 6-digit code).
2 Send Files from This Phone
Tap the big upload box or Browse Files / Photos, select any file, photo, or PDF, and tap Send to PC. It uploads instantly.
3 Automatic Saving on PC
Your PC automatically receives files straight into Downloads\Submitt. No manual clicking needed!
4 Share from Drive / WhatsApp (App)
Install the V-Share Android App. In Google Drive, Photos, or Files, tap regular Share → choose Send to PC (V-Share)!
