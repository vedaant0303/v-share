📖 How to Use V-Share

1️⃣ Connect Your Phone

Scan the on-screen QR code with your phone camera, or enter your 6-digit Private Pairing Code on your phone. Your phone will immediately connect with a green indicator!

2️⃣ Receive Files Automatically (Zero Clicks)

Whenever you drop photos, videos, or documents from your phone, they are saved directly into your computer's Downloads\Submitt folder. Click "📂 Choose Folder" to pick any other folder.

3️⃣ Silent Background Mode (No Open Tabs Needed!)

You do not need to keep this window open on your taskbar! V-Share runs silently in the background on your PC. You can close this window completely — when a file arrives, a Windows notification will alert you!

4️⃣ Multiple PCs on the Same Wi-Fi

If you have 2 PCs in your house, click the 🎲 button next to the pairing code on PC 2. This gives PC 2 its own unique code. On your phone, you can switch between PC 1 and PC 2 with just 1 tap!
